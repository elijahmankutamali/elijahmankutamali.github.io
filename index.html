About Me

Service, Leadership and Lifelong Learning

A developing educator and youth leader committed to education, public service and community progress.

I am Elijah Mankutam Ali, an educator, communicator, civil activist, student leader and Wikimedian from the North East Region of Ghana. My journey has been shaped by education, student leadership, Christian ministry, teaching and community service.

I am passionate about giving young people meaningful opportunities to participate in decision-making and development. I value responsible leadership, unity, teamwork, discipline and service.

Through my academic journey and service roles, I aim to encourage young people to learn, lead with integrity and contribute to building stronger communities.
